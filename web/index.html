      Device

      Join the caller

      1. Power the caller. Give it about 10 seconds.
      2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi TurkeyCaller with password gobble123 (unless you changed them under Settings).
      3. Open this page. If the phone says "no internet", stay connected anyway.

      Two phones can be connected at once. Both stay in step.

      Pair the speaker

      1. Turn the speaker on and put it in pairing mode.
      2. Open Speaker and tap Search for speakers. It takes up to about 25 seconds and stops any call playing.
      3. Tap Use next to your speaker. The caller remembers it, even after a restart.
      4. To switch speakers, search again and tap Use on the new one. No need to forget the old one first. Forget speaker (tap twice) only clears the saved speaker.

      Play calls

      • Tap a call to play it. Tap it again, or Stop, to stop.
      • Repeat loops the call until you tap Stop. The switch is shared by both phones.
      • Volume: slider, or the − and + buttons. It is remembered.
      • If the speaker went to sleep, the first tap wakes it. Allow a few seconds.
      • Turkey, Moose and Deer calls have their own tab (Deer shows once there are deer calls). Moose and deer calls are stored on the SD card, so they only show when the card is in.
      • Gobble and putt are under "Use with care": they can draw hunters or spook birds.
      • The moon button switches to a dim red screen that saves your night vision.

      Settings

      • Wi-Fi name and password (8 to 63 plain letters, digits and symbols, no accents): changing either restarts the caller. Rejoin the new Wi-Fi afterwards.
      • Device name shows at the top of this page. No Wi-Fi restart.
      • Every change shows on both phones at once. If the other phone saved first, yours asks you to review before saving.
      • Change the factory password before you rely on the caller.

      The box's buttons

      Yellow: previous call, or volume up while a call plays. Blue: next call, or volume down.

      Green: play the call on the screen. Hold 1 s while it plays: Repeat on or off.

      Red: next animal (Turkey, Moose, Deer), or stop while a call plays.

      The screen goes dark after 30 s; the first press only wakes it. Calls started on the box show here too, and the other way round.

      Phone-free mode

      On the box, with the call list showing, hold the red button for 3 seconds. The caller restarts with Wi-Fi off: no phone can join, and the battery lasts longer. Pick and play calls with the box's buttons.

      Hold red 3 s again to turn Wi-Fi back on. Holding the board's BOOT button 5 s also turns it back on, and keeps your Wi-Fi name and password.

      Reset to defaults

      Forgot the Wi-Fi password? With the caller powered on and running, hold the BOOT button on the board for 5 seconds. It restarts with Wi-Fi TurkeyCaller, password gobble123 and the default device name.

      It keeps the speaker, volume and sounds. A short tap does nothing. Do not hold BOOT while plugging in power: that puts the board in programming mode.

      Troubleshooting

      "Speaker did not answer"
      Turn the speaker on, bring it close, and tap the call again. Still nothing: Speaker, search, and Use it again.
      "Searching for speakers"
      A search is running (up to about 25 seconds). Calls wait until it ends.
      "No speaker connected"
      Pair one under Speaker.
      No sound, speaker connected
      Raise the volume on this page and on the speaker itself.
      "Lost the caller"
      Check your phone is still on the caller's Wi-Fi. The page reconnects by itself.
      Crackle or dropouts
      Move the speaker closer and keep it clear of obstacles. Search only when needed.
      Page will not open
      Rejoin the caller's Wi-Fi, then try 192.168.4.1. Still stuck: power off and on.
